# Suggestions

Lets members submit suggestions with <code>/suggestion</code>. They get posted to a dedicated channel where everyone can vote, instead of getting lost in general chat.

Configure under **Settings > Suggestions**:

- **Enable/disable** the module.
- **Suggestions channel** - where submitted suggestions are posted.
- **Editor role** - members with this role can approve or decline suggestions. Members with the <b>Manage Server</b> permission can always do this.

How writing, voting and deciding works is explained in <a href="Setup-Suggestions.md"/>.
