# Notes

Quick sticky notes and shared pads, for the kind of scratch information that doesn't belong in the Wiki but shouldn't just live in someone's head either.

<note>
Needs the <code>notes</code> module enabled. <code>create_notes</code> lets you make your own personal notes; <code>manage_notes</code> is needed to create, edit or delete shared team notes.
</note>

There are two kinds:

- **Personal notes** - only visible to you, for your own reminders and scratchpad thoughts.
- **Shared team notes** - visible to the whole team, useful for a running list that several people contribute to (e.g. "things to bring up at the next meeting").

Notes are intentionally lightweight - no folders, no tags. If something grows into actual reference material worth keeping long-term, move it into the <a href="Wiki-Panel.md">Wiki</a> instead.
