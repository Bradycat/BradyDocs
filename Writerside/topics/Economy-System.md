# Economy System

<p>With <code>/enablefeature Economy System</code> you can easily activate the coins on your Guild.</p>
<p>Members collect coins and can spend them in the <a href="Casino-Games.md">casino</a>, on saves in the <a href="Counting-Game.md">counting game</a> or in games like <a href="Tic-Tac-Toe.md">Tic-Tac-Toe</a>. Coins are counted per server.</p>

## Earning coins

- <code>/reward</code> - your daily reward of 150 coins, once every 24 hours.
- Winning games such as <a href="Setup-Guess-Number.md">Guess Number</a>, <a href="CityGuessrChild.md">CityGuessr</a> or <a href="Tic-Tac-Toe.md">Tic-Tac-Toe</a>.
- Getting coins from other members with <code>/pay</code>.

## Commands

<chapter title="/coins" id="coins" collapsible="true">
    <p>Shows how many coins you have. Add the <code>user</code> option to see the coins of someone else.</p>
</chapter>
<chapter title="/pay" id="pay" collapsible="true">
    <p>Sends coins to another member. Enter the member and the amount.</p>
</chapter>
<chapter title="/cointop" id="cointop" collapsible="true">
    <p>Shows the 10 members with the most coins on the server.</p>
</chapter>
<chapter title="/reward" id="reward" collapsible="true">
    <p>Collects your daily reward of 150 coins. If you already collected it, the bot tells you how long you have to wait.</p>
</chapter>
<chapter title="/admincoins" id="admincoins" collapsible="true">
    <p>Lets admins <b>add</b>, <b>remove</b> or <b>set</b> the coins of a member, e.g. to hand out prizes. Needs the <b>Administrator</b> permission.</p>
    <note><p>This command is part of the closed beta.</p></note>
</chapter>

<tip>
<p>You can also switch the economy on and off in the dashboard, see <a href="Games-Module.md"/>.</p>
</tip>
