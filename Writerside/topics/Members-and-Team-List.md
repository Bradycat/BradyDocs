# Members and Team List

**Team List** gives you an overview of everyone on the team - who's active, who's away, and what roles they hold - without having to open Discord.

<note>
This is a module and needs to be enabled first, see <a href="Modules-and-Settings.md"/>.
</note>

## Members

Under **Mitglieder** (Members) you can see everyone who has been added to the team, along with their assigned roles. Someone with `manage_members` can remove a member from the team here - this only removes them from Team-Panel, it does not kick them from Discord.

Adding members generally happens automatically: the first time someone from your Discord server logs into the panel, they show up here (assuming the bot is present and the team exists).

## Team List

The Team List view combines member data with whatever is relevant elsewhere in the panel - for example, if <a href="Absences.md">Absences</a> is enabled, you'll see who's currently marked as away right in this list, so you don't have to check two different places before assigning someone a task.
