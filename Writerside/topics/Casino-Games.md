# Casino Games
<warning>
    Important: the casino plays with coins, so the <a href="Economy-System.md">Economy System</a> has to be active as well (<code>/enablefeature Economy System</code>).
</warning>
<procedure title="" id="casinogames">
    <step>
        <p>As we currently have no settings options for this game mode, it is sufficient to simply execute <code>/enablefeature Casino System</code></p>
    </step>
    <step>
        <p>Press <shortcut>Enter</shortcut> to send the message!</p>
    </step>
    <step>
        <p>Done! Then simply go to a channel where commands are active and enter <code>/slots</code> or <code>/coinflip</code>!</p>
    </step>
</procedure>

## Commands

<chapter title="/slots" id="slots" collapsible="true">
    <p>Play the slot machine. Your bet has to be between 100 and 100,000 coins. Depending on the symbols you get back more or less than you bet.</p>
</chapter>
<chapter title="/coinflip" id="coinflip" collapsible="true">
    <p>Bet on <b>Heads</b> or <b>Tails</b> (up to 1,000,000 coins). If the coin lands on your side, you win your bet on top - otherwise you lose it.</p>
</chapter>
<chapter title="/casinotop" id="casinotop" collapsible="true">
    <p>Shows the 10 members with the highest casino profit this week. The list starts over every Monday at midnight.</p>
    <note><p>The weekly leaderboard is part of the closed beta and has to be switched on in the dashboard under <b>Games</b>, see <a href="Games-Module.md"/>.</p></note>
</chapter>
