# CityGuessr

<warning>
The feature is currently still in closed beta
</warning>

<p>The bot shows a picture of a city and you have to pick the right one out of four answers.</p>

<procedure title="" id="cityguessr">
    <step>
        <p>As we currently have no settings options for this game mode, it is sufficient to simply execute <code>/enablefeature CityGuessr</code></p>
    </step>
    <step>
        <p>Press <shortcut>Enter</shortcut> to send the message!</p>
    </step>
    <step>
        <p>Done! Then simply go to a channel where commands are active and enter <code>/cityguessr start</code>!</p>
    </step>
</procedure>

## Playing
You have 30 seconds to click the right city. Every member can only play one round at a time.

<note>Also works with the <a href="Economy-System.md">Economy system</a> switched off</note>

If the Economy System is active, CityGuessr is played for coins: a right answer earns you coins (the amount depends on the city), a wrong answer costs 100 coins. You need at least 100 coins to start a round.

## Commands

<chapter title="/cityguessr start" id="cityguessr-start" collapsible="true">
    <p>Starts a new round.</p>
</chapter>
<chapter title="/cityguessr stats" id="cityguessr-stats" collapsible="true">
    <p>Shows your CityGuessr stats: right and wrong answers, rounds played and your current streak.</p>
</chapter>
