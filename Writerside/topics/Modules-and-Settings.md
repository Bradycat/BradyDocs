# Modules and Settings

Not every team needs every feature. Modules let you turn whole sections of the panel on or off, so the sidebar only shows what your team actually uses.

<note>
Managing modules requires <code>manage_modules</code>.
</note>

Turning a module off hides it from the sidebar for everyone, but doesn't delete its data - turn it back on later and everything is exactly where you left it.

## Team settings

Beyond modules, the team's general settings cover things like the display language for the panel and the accent color used throughout your team's pages. These are cosmetic/organizational and don't require any special permission beyond `manage_modules` in most cases.
