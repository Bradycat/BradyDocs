# Level System

Members earn levels by being active in text channels. Configure it under **Settings > Level System**.

- **Enable/disable** the whole system.
- **Announcement channel** - where level-up messages are posted.
- **No-XP channels** - channels where messages don't count toward XP (useful for bot-command or spam channels).
- **Level rewards** - assign a role to be given automatically once a member reaches a specific level. Add as many level/role pairs as you like.

<note>
Level rewards are additive - reaching level 10 doesn't remove the role you got at level 5, unless you've set it up that way in Discord's own role hierarchy.
</note>
