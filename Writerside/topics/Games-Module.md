# Games

One settings page covers all of Bradycat's mini-games and fun features. Configure under **Settings > Games**.

<deflist type="medium">
    <def title="Minecraft">
        Enable Minecraft player/server lookups. There's a separate toggle for Hypixel-specific data if your community plays on Hypixel.
    </def>
    <def title="Economy, Casino, Games">
        Three independent switches. <b>Economy</b> turns on the coin system (<code>/pay</code>, <code>/cointop</code>). <b>Casino</b> unlocks <code>/slots</code> and <code>/coinflip</code> (requires Economy). <b>Games</b> is the general switch for the smaller game commands.
    </def>
    <def title="Tic-Tac-Toe">
        Enable/disable, and pick the channel it's playable in.
    </def>
    <def title="Guess the Number">
        Enable/disable, and pick the channel.
    </def>
    <def title="CityGuessr (GeoGuessr-style)">
        Enable/disable, and pick the channel.
    </def>
    <def title="Counting Game">
        Enable/disable, pick the channel, and configure: a role assigned when someone breaks the count, whether wrong "math" answers are allowed, and whether only plain numbers are accepted (turning this off allows normal chat messages in the channel without them counting as mistakes).
    </def>
</deflist>
