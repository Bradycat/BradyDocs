# Personal Dashboard

After logging in, you land on your personal dashboard - a list of every server where you have Discord admin permissions and where the bot is present. Click any of them to open that server's dashboard.

<note>
Don't see a server you expect? Either the bot isn't in it yet, or you don't have admin permissions there. Both are checked live against Discord, not against some separate BradyCat permission list.
</note>

## Changelog

The **Changelog** page lists what's changed in the bot recently - new features, fixes, adjustments - so you don't have to guess whether something you're seeing is new or you just never noticed it before.
