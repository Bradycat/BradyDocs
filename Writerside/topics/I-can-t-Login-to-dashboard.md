# I can`t Login to dashboard

<p>You seem to have landed here because you can't log in to the dashboard, this can have many reasons.</p>

<h1>Blocked cookies</h1>
<p>The dashboard needs a cookie to keep you logged in. We only use technically necessary cookies, but if your browser or an extension (ad blocker, privacy add-on) blocks cookies for our site, the login cannot finish and you end up logged out again.</p>
<p>Allow cookies for our site, or delete the cookies of our site and try again. Here is a contribution to google, as it is with Google Chrome. <a href="https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/32050?hl=en">Click me</a></p>

<h1>Your server is missing</h1>
<p>If you can log in but don't see your server, check <a href="Personal-Dashboard.md"/>: only servers where you have the Administrator permission and where the bot is present are shown.</p>

<h1>Errors on our part</h1>

<p>If it hasn't fixed the problem, please let us know <a href="https://discord.bradycat.de">on our Discord</a> so that we can get to the bottom of the problem.</p>
<tip>Then create a ticket on the Discord!</tip>
