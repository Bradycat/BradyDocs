# Private Voice Channels

Lets members create their own temporary private voice channel on demand, instead of you pre-creating a fixed number of voice rooms.

Configure under **Settings > Voice**:

- **Enable/disable** the module.
- **Trigger channel** - the voice channel members join to have a private one created for them.
- **Category** - where the newly created private channels are placed.

The private channel is removed automatically once everyone leaves it.
