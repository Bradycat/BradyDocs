# Private Voice Channels

Lets members create their own temporary voice channel on demand, instead of you pre-creating a fixed number of voice rooms.

Configure under **Settings > Voice**:

- **Enable/disable** the module.
- **Trigger channel** - the voice channel members join to have their own one created for them.
- **Category** - where the newly created channels are placed.

A personal channel is removed automatically as soon as its owner leaves it. How members manage their channel with <code>/pchannel</code> is explained in <a href="Private-Voice-Channels.md"/>.
